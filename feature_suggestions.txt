MCA MOD - FEATURE SUGGESTIONS
==============================

Based on analysis of the codebase, here are feature ideas organized by category:

---

RELATIONSHIPS & SOCIAL
=======================

High Impact:
- Divorce System - Allow married couples to divorce based on low hearts/compatibility
- Jealousy Mechanics - Villagers get jealous if spouse flirts with others
- Best Friends System - Deep friendships that unlock special dialogues/quests
- Feuds & Rivalries - Villagers can dislike each other, refuse to work together
- Anniversary Celebrations - Annual marriage anniversary events with gifts

Medium Impact:
- Dating Stage - Intermediate step between courting and marriage with unique interactions
- Love Letters - Villagers can write/send romantic letters to each other
- Double Dates - Take your spouse and another couple on activities together
- Breakup Mechanics - End relationships before marriage with relationship penalty
- Friendship Bracelets - Craftable items to cement friendships

---

GENETICS & APPEARANCE
======================

High Impact:
- Eye Color Genetics - Inherit eye color from parents (currently basic)
- Body Type Variations - Muscular, slender, stocky builds affecting stats
- Aging Visual Effects - Wrinkles, gray hair as villagers age
- Scars & Marks - Combat/life events leave visible marks
- Twins Visual Similarity - Twins look nearly identical

Medium Impact:
- Freckles Gene - Some villagers spawn with freckles
- Tattoos - Unlockable cosmetic customization for adults
- Facial Hair Genetics - Male villagers inherit beard styles
- Heterochromia Trait - Rare trait for different colored eyes
- Height Extremes - Very tall/short variants beyond normal range

AI & DIALOGUE
=============

High Impact:
- Memory System Enhancement - Villagers remember past conversations better
- Emotional Responses - AI reacts to player's emotional tone
- Dynamic Quest Generation - AI creates unique quests based on villager needs
- Context-Aware Responses - AI knows about recent village events
- Voice Personality Matching - Different AI voices for different personalities

Medium Impact:
- Gossip System - Villagers share information about other villagers
- Story Continuation - AI remembers and continues stories across sessions
- Mood-Based Dialogue - Different responses based on villager's current mood
- Cultural Knowledge - Villagers know about their profession/background
- Player Reputation Awareness - AI mentions your standing in village

VILLAGE & ECONOMY
==================

High Impact:
- Village Festivals - Seasonal events (harvest festival, winter ball, etc.)
- Economy System - Village-wide currency, shops, markets
- Village Quests - Collective goals (build library, defeat raid boss)
- Village Specializations - Villages focus on farming, mining, trading, etc.
- Dynamic Population - Villagers migrate between villages based on conditions

Medium Impact:
- Village Council - Democratic voting on village decisions
- Shared Resources - Village-wide storage and crafting stations
- Trade Routes - Villages trade with each other automatically
- Village Reputation Tiers - Unlock perks as village grows (bronze→silver→gold)
- Village Newspaper - Weekly printed summary of events
